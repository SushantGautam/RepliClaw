## Why (read `docs/COMPETITION_STRATEGY.md` §G1)
`protocol.py::_fulfill_need` today CHOOSES a role and INVOKES the follow-up directly in the same process — that is central dispatch, not decentralized claim/fulfil. The vendored ScienceClaw already ships `artifacts/needs.py` (`NeedItem`, `NeedsSignal`, `NeedsSignalBroadcaster`) and `artifacts/reactor.py` (`ArtifactReactor`, `register_peer`, `scan_needs`, `react_to_needs`, `_mark_need_consumed`) plus `pressure.py` (`score_need`, `NeedRef`). `scienceclaw_adapter.py` already imports these. The whole point of the competition is **decentralized agency (20%)** — so the design must lean on these primitives, not reinvent them.
