## Critical 1 — P1 success/falsification rule appears sign-reversed AND comparator-mismatched
At \`docs/experiments/PREREG-2026-10-v1.1.md\` §2 P1: "escrow value, RQ1/S5 vs S4" expects S5 matches or beats S4, but its literal decision rule says *"S5 succeeds P1 iff bootstrap 95% CI upper bound of the 10-run interim difference (S5 minus S3 on M1) is < 0, i.e. the entire CI is below zero"*. For higher-is-better correctness, an upper confidence bound below zero on S5−S3 indicates S5 is **worse** than S3. The comparison is also S3, not the claimed S4. The same string is intentionally repeated in §8.1 stopping rule (the earlier judge explicitly required consistent wording but may not have assessed direction). This cannot logically be the success criterion for S5 meeting or beating S4. Treat as an **URGENT science-judge BLOCKER**, not mere prose: re-derive exact estimand, polarity, null, CI, favorable direction, interim/final rules and comparator arm; reviewer must sign a versioned prereg amendment if it is wrong, **before** any live outcomes. Do not relabel negative evidence positive.
